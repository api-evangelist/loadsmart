For enterprise shippers, AI agents take over the repetitive tasks (emails, phone calls, manual data entry) with 100% resolution guaranteed by Loadsmart's in-house freight experts. CHICAGO, September 17, 2026 ( PR Newswire ) - Loadsmart AI launches a set of AI agents that execute freight work end to end inside the systems enterprise shippers already run. The agents come backed by the company's own freight operators, who close out any task the agents don't resolve.
